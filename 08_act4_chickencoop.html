Found at the Chicken Coop — this letter has some words that are the wrong way round!

Daddy Ooo Ooo was guarding the chicken coop when the thief came past — but he was so excited that he wrote some words that mean the opposite of what he meant! Your job is to read it carefully, cross out each wrong word in red and write the opposite word on the line next to it.

📋   Your Instructions

There are 5 opposite words hidden in the letter below. Each one is underlined in red. Cross it out and write the opposite on the blank line beside it. When you have fixed all five, read the letter again — it will tell you something very important!

📜   Letter from Daddy Ooo Ooo — Official Witness Account
Dear Agent Nyxie,
Hello! It is me, Daddy Ooo Ooo! I have been sitting by the chicken coop all day because I saw something very exciting happen!

Last night, a very sneaky big creature crept past me in the light . It was carrying a bag that was full of something dull and sparkly — I think it was the Crown Jewels! The creature ran very slow and ran straight through the garden and disappeared!

I tried to shout "OOO OOO OOO!" but nobody came. So I wrote you this letter instead because you are the best detective I know.

The Superintendent has heard about all your brilliant work today, Agent Nyxie, and she is waiting for you in the dining room with a very special surprise. You had better go there right away!

Good luck! Ooo ooo ooo!
Daddy Ooo Ooo
Daddy Ooo Ooo · Bravest Stuffy at the Coop
