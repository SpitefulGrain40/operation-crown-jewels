Garage · Witness Statement from Geraferty 🦒

📝   Witness Statement
Agent Nyxie,
My name is Geraferty, and I am a very tall giraffe. I was in the garage yesterday, standing on my back legs trying to reach the high shelf (I do this quite often — helps me stay strong). I saw someone very sneaky creeping past the climbing wall!

The suspicious creature was carrying a big bag. It looked heavy, and it was shimmering in the light. I'm quite sure it was the Crown Jewels! The creature moved very quietly, almost like it didn't want to be seen.

I tried to call out, but I got distracted trying not to fall off my back legs. By the time I shouted, the creature was already gone, running towards the garden.

I hope this helps you catch the thief!
Geraferty 🦒
