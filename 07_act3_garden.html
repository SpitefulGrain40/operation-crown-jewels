📋   Note from Lamby — As dictated to Agent Daddy
Dearest Agent Nyxie,

Oh, I am ever so pleased you have found me! I have been out here all by myself and it has been very frightening indeed. I was enjoying a peaceful evening in the Secret Garden — just doing some quiet grazing, as one does — when I heard the most tremendous rustling in the bushes.

I hid behind a plant pot immediately (I am very brave like that) and watched as a sneaky creature crept through the garden, clutching something that glittered and sparkled. It moved so quickly I could not see its face — but I did notice that it left behind a very important clue somewhere nearby. I have been guarding this garden ever since, waiting for a proper detective to come and investigate.

Your job, Agent Nyxie, is to look very carefully all around this garden and record your observations on the Crime Scene Evidence Sheet — that is what detectives do! You must draw and describe three things you can see, hear, or find out here. The garden holds its secrets for those who look closely enough.

And once you have done that — I have a riddle for you. Solve it, and you will know exactly where to go next. 🌿

Lamby
Lamby · Bravest Garden Resident · Official Stuffy Witness
