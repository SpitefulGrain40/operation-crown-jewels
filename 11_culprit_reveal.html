Dear Agent Nyxie,

It was us. It was all of us. We are very, very sorry.

We did not expect anyone as brilliant as you to be on the case. You followed every single clue, solved every single puzzle, and you even braved the laser vault — which, we must admit, was extremely impressive.

We have learned our lesson. The Crown Jewels belong to the Secret Garden Kingdom, and we promise — on all of our fluff — never to borrow them again without asking.

Please don't be too cross with us. We are only stuffies.
